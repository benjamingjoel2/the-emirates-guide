Stand at the foot of the green mound on Sheikh Zayed Road and look up. A silver ring curves above you, its skin carved with flowing Arabic script, and through the empty oval at its centre you can see the sky and the towers behind.

That view is still yours to enjoy. What has changed is what happens inside. If you are planning a trip and the Museum of the Future is on your list, the most important thing to know is this: the museum is temporarily closed while it is completely reimagined, and its official help page says it is [set to reopen in the first quarter of 2027](https://museumofthefuture.ae/en/get-help). Here is what that means for your plans, what will be different when the doors open again, and how to make the most of the building in the meantime.

## Is the Museum of the Future open?

Not for its main exhibitions. According to the museum, it [temporarily closed its doors to visitors at the beginning of September 2026](https://museumofthefuture.ae/en/get-help) to prepare an entirely new experience. The homepage notes that while the exhibitions are unavailable, the [online retail store and selected experiences remain open](https://museumofthefuture.ae/en).

The help page adds that some spaces [may remain accessible for selected activities and services](https://museumofthefuture.ae/en/get-help), and that selected events and programmes may continue during the closure. If you see an event advertised, check the museum's official channels to confirm what is on and whether it is open to the public.

The closure is not permanent. The museum describes it as lasting [only a few months](https://museumofthefuture.ae/en/get-help), timed so that the reopening coincides with its fifth anniversary.

### What if your trip is before reopening?

Then treat the Museum of the Future as an architectural stop rather than a museum visit. Allow time to walk around the building, see the calligraphy up close and take photographs from across the road. It sits well alongside other Downtown and Trade Centre sights, and you can fill the rest of the day with places that are open.

### What if your trip is after reopening?

Keep an eye on the official site. The museum says tickets for the renewed experience will be released [closer to the reopening](https://museumofthefuture.ae/en/get-help). Expect strong demand in the first weeks, and book as soon as you can once dates are confirmed.

## What will be different when it reopens

The renewal is not a light refresh. The museum says the closure is a chance to [reimagine it from the ground up](https://museumofthefuture.ae/en/get-help), with a new narrative, new exhibitions, new spaces and a redesigned visitor journey.

A few points stand out from the official answers:

- **The old exhibitions will not return.** The museum says the current exhibitions and experiences [will not come back in their previous form](https://museumofthefuture.ae/en/get-help). If you saw it before, the next visit will be a different journey.
- **More interaction.** The renewed museum aims to make visitors [active participants](https://museumofthefuture.ae/en/get-help), exploring ideas and shaping their own route, with a more immersive story, new exhibitions and labs.
- **The building stays the same.** The museum confirms the [exterior will not change](https://museumofthefuture.ae/en/get-help); the work focuses on what happens inside.
- **Accessibility remains a priority.** The museum says the renewed space will include [spaces, services and experiences](https://museumofthefuture.ae/en/get-help) designed for a comfortable visit for People of Determination.

### What kind of museum is it?

Do not expect glass cases and ancient relics. The museum describes itself as a place that combines [exhibition, immersive theatre and themed attraction](https://museumofthefuture.ae/en/about-us), with each floor of its first edition designed like a film set from a possible future. Its stated aim is to be hopeful about the future while staying honest about the problems of the present. It is an initiative of the [Dubai Future Foundation](https://museumofthefuture.ae/en/about-us), the body set up to plan and shape the emirate's future.

That gives a sense of the tone, even before the new exhibitions are revealed: interactive, theatrical and built around ideas rather than objects.

## Reading the building from the outside

Even with the doors closed to exhibition visitors, the Museum of the Future rewards a slow look. Few buildings in Dubai say so much on their surface.

### The shape

The building has three parts, each with a meaning. The museum explains that the [green hill represents the earth](https://museumofthefuture.ae/en/get-help), the torus-shaped building represents humankind's ability to innovate, and the void at the centre represents the unknown. Walk around it and you will notice how the void frames different slices of the city depending on where you stand.

### The calligraphy

The facade is far more than decoration. The museum says the building ["speaks Arabic"](https://museumofthefuture.ae/en/the-building): its surface is a canvas for sayings of the Ruler of Dubai on the future, innovation and legacy, rendered in Arabic calligraphy. Look closely and the letters seem to shift as the light moves across the steel through the day.

### The numbers behind the shine

The facade is made of stainless steel in [1,024 pieces](https://museumofthefuture.ae/en/the-building), produced with a robot-assisted process and covering a surface area of 17,600 square metres. The building stands 77 metres tall. The museum also says it receives solar energy from [arrays mounted nearby](https://museumofthefuture.ae/en/get-help).

### Photographing it

Photographers tend to favour two moments: soft early light, when the steel turns warm, and dusk, when the city lights come on around it. Keep your photography to the building and the street. The UK Foreign Office warns visitors [not to photograph people without their permission](https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/united-arab-emirates/safety-and-security), and that applies here as much as anywhere.

## How to fit it into a Dubai day

Because a visit to the exterior takes far less time than a full museum visit, it pairs easily with other sights.

### A futures and views day

Start with the Museum of the Future exterior in the morning, then head to Downtown for the observation decks. Our guide to [visiting the Burj Khalifa](/stories/burj-khalifa-visit-guide/) covers which deck to pick and when to book. Finish at the Dubai Frame, which tells the story of the city's growth from fishing village to modern metropolis; see our [Dubai Frame guide](/stories/dubai-frame-guide/).

### A past and future day

For contrast, pair the building's vision of tomorrow with the city's past. A morning in the wind-tower lanes of the old town, covered in our [Old Dubai walking guide](/stories/old-dubai-walking-guide/), followed by a late afternoon at the Museum of the Future makes the distance Dubai has travelled feel real.

### With children

Children who are disappointed that the inside is closed often still enjoy spotting letters in the facade and walking around the mound. Our [Dubai with children guide](/stories/dubai-with-kids/) has more family ideas that work while the museum is being renewed. For the wider city, start at our [Dubai destination page](/destinations/dubai/).

### Practical notes

- **Dress:** the building sits in a business district, and modest, comfortable clothes are the easiest choice. See [what to wear in the UAE](/stories/what-to-wear-uae-etiquette/) for a fuller guide.
- **Heat:** you will be outdoors the whole time, so go early or late in the warmer months and carry water.
- **Check before you go:** closures, events and reopening plans can change, so look at the official site shortly before your visit.

## Quick answers

**Is the Museum of the Future closed?** Yes, temporarily. Its main exhibitions closed at the beginning of September 2026 while the museum is completely reimagined.

**When does the Museum of the Future reopen?** The museum says it is set to reopen in the first quarter of 2027, coinciding with its fifth anniversary.

**Can I buy tickets now?** Not yet for the renewed museum. Tickets will be available closer to the reopening.

**Will the exhibitions be the same as before?** No. The museum says the previous exhibitions will not return in their old form and will be replaced by new ones.

**Can I still see the building?** Yes. The exterior is not changing, and the online store and selected experiences remain open during the closure.

## Questions to ask your operator

- Has the Museum of the Future announced a firm reopening date or ticket release since we booked?
- Are any events or spaces at the museum open to visitors during our dates?
- When is the best time of day on our dates to see and photograph the building?
- Can you plan a route that combines the museum exterior with open attractions nearby without long waits in the heat?
- If the museum reopens during our trip, can you help us get timed tickets as soon as they are released?
