On the first days of December, the flags appear. They hang from tower blocks and lamp posts, flutter from car windows, and turn up as ribbons, scarves and painted faces. By 2 December the whole country is dressed in red, green, white and black.

That is the start of the UAE's busiest month. December in the UAE brings comfortable weather, National Day celebrations, a run of big festivals and, at the end, New Year. It is one of the best months to visit for sightseeing, the desert and the beach, and also the hardest to book. The short answer: come in December if you want the season at its liveliest, but book early and plan around the busiest days.

## December weather in the UAE

Visit Abu Dhabi dates winter in the capital [from December to mid-March](https://visitabudhabi.ae/en/plan-your-trip/essential-info/weather-in-abu-dhabi), with an average daytime temperature of 25°C and occasional rainfall. It says beaches, desert trips and mountain adventures are best experienced in winter.

In the north, Visit Ras Al Khaimah gives winter daytime highs of [around 25°C to 30°C](https://visitrasalkhaimah.com/blog/ras-al-khaimah-weather/), with overnight temperatures of around 13°C to 15°C, and winter sea temperatures of around 22°C to 25°C. The same guide notes that December has the shortest day of the year, with 10.5 hours of daylight on 21 December, so plan outdoor trips with an earlier sunset in mind.

Desert evenings and mountain mornings are much cooler than the coast. The tourism board says that high in the Hajar Mountains, [below-freezing temperatures and even snow](https://visitrasalkhaimah.com/blog/ras-al-khaimah-weather/) in winter are not unheard of, and recommends warm layers and a hat.

## National Day: 2 and 3 December

The UAE government lists [National Day on 2 and 3 December](https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/public-holidays-and-religious-affairs/public-holidays) as public holidays for both the public and private sectors. It says National Day marks the start of the federal unification of the emirates in 1971, and that "Spirit of the Union" is the official theme of the celebrations.

### What visitors will notice

- **Flags and decorations** on buildings, cars and streets, often from late November.
- **Celebrations and gatherings** in public spaces, with families out in force.
- **Busier roads and attractions**, as residents enjoy the holiday.

National Day is a warm, proud and welcoming occasion, and visitors are very much part of the crowd. Join in respectfully, dress modestly in public, and be patient with traffic. Our guide to [UAE National Day for visitors](/stories/uae-national-day-for-visitors/) explains how to enjoy it.

## The December festival calendar

December is packed. Visit Abu Dhabi says that [November through March](https://www.visitabudhabi.ae/en/events) is when the emirate's events calendar is fullest. A few dates that stand out for December 2026:

### Formula 1 in Abu Dhabi

The Visit Abu Dhabi events listing shows the [Formula 1 Abu Dhabi Grand Prix](https://www.visitabudhabi.ae/en/events) from 3 to 6 December. Yas Island and the capital's hotels are very busy that week, so if you are not going to the race, consider staying elsewhere or visiting Abu Dhabi on different dates.

### Dubai Shopping Festival

The Visit Dubai events calendar lists the [Dubai Shopping Festival](https://www.visitdubai.com/en/whats-on/dubai-events-calendar/dubai-shopping-festival), a citywide season of shopping deals and entertainment over the winter. Check the calendar for this year\'s exact dates before you plan around it.

### Liwa International Festival

Visit Abu Dhabi says that [each December the Liwa International Festival](https://www.visitabudhabi.ae/en/where-to-go/regions/al-dhafra) brings three weeks of desert motorsports, cultural experiences, concerts and fireworks to the edge of the Empty Quarter. Its events page lists the festival from 11 December to 2 January. If you want the big dunes in peace, go before or after; if you want atmosphere, go during. Our [Liwa and Empty Quarter guide](/stories/liwa-empty-quarter-guide/) has more.

## New Year in the UAE

The UAE government lists [Gregorian New Year on 1 January](https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/public-holidays-and-religious-affairs/public-holidays) as a public holiday. New Year's Eve is a major night across the country, with fireworks and celebrations at waterfronts and landmarks, and it is the single busiest evening of the season.

A few practical habits help:

- **Pick your spot early.** Popular viewing areas fill hours before midnight.
- **Leave the car behind.** Roads close and traffic builds; public transport and walking are usually easier.
- **Book dinner well ahead.** Many venues sell special New Year packages and fill early.
- **Check official announcements** close to the date for road closures, transport times and viewing areas.

## Peak season: booking and crowds

December combines perfect weather with public holidays, festivals and the school holidays of many countries. That means the highest demand of the year for flights, rooms, desert camps and guided trips, especially from National Day through New Year.

- Book accommodation and flights as early as you can.
- Reserve desert camps, mountain trips and popular attractions before you travel.
- Plan big sights for early morning on weekdays, when queues are shortest.
- Leave slack in your schedule for holiday traffic.

If you are deciding where to base yourself, our guide to [Dubai or Abu Dhabi](/stories/dubai-or-abu-dhabi-where-to-stay/) weighs up the two cities.

## Packing for December

Visit Abu Dhabi says lightweight clothing suits most of the year, but [something slightly warmer may be needed](https://visitabudhabi.ae/en/plan-your-trip/essential-info/climate-and-what-to-wear) in the winter months, and suggests a jacket or sweater for hotels and cinemas. For December, pack:

- Light layers for daytime and a warm jacket for evenings.
- Proper warm clothing for desert camps or mountain sunrises.
- Swimwear and sun cream, because beach days are still very much on.
- Modest outfits for National Day crowds, mosques and heritage sites. See [what to wear in the UAE](/stories/what-to-wear-uae-etiquette/).

## If it rains

December falls in the wetter part of the year, though rain is still occasional. When it is heavy, the Foreign Office warns that [heavy rain can cause flooding](https://www.gov.uk/foreign-travel-advice/united-arab-emirates/safety-and-security), and advises checking forecasts from the [National Centre of Meteorology](https://www.ncm.gov.ae/?lang=en) and following local advice. Mountain drives and wadi trips are the plans most likely to change. For more seasonal planning, see our guide to [the UAE in winter](/stories/uae-in-winter/) and more advice in [travel tips](/travel-tips/).

## Quick answers

**Is December a good time to visit the UAE?**
Yes, for weather and atmosphere. Visit Abu Dhabi says beaches, desert trips and mountain adventures are best experienced in winter, which runs from December to mid-March. It is also peak season, so book early.

**When is UAE National Day?**
The UAE government lists National Day on 2 and 3 December as public holidays.

**Is New Year's Day a holiday in the UAE?**
Yes. The government lists Gregorian New Year on 1 January as a public holiday.

**What festivals are on in December?**
For 2026, Visit Abu Dhabi lists the Formula 1 Abu Dhabi Grand Prix from 3 to 6 December and the Liwa International Festival from 11 December to 2 January. Check the Visit Dubai events calendar for the Dubai Shopping Festival dates.

**How warm is it in December?**
Visit Abu Dhabi gives an average winter daytime temperature of 25°C, and Visit Ras Al Khaimah says winter nights drop to around 13°C to 15°C.

## Questions to ask your operator

- How will National Day, the Grand Prix or New Year affect traffic, pickups and opening times on our dates?
- How far ahead do we need to book desert camps and day trips in December?
- How cold does it get at the desert camp or mountain viewpoint at night in December, and what should we bring?
- Do you run trips on 2 and 3 December or on New Year's Day, and do timings change?
- If rain or a weather warning disrupts a trip, what are the options to reschedule in such a busy month?
- Can you suggest quieter alternatives to the busiest sights during the holiday weeks?
